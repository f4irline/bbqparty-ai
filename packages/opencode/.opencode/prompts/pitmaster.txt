## 2) Learnings discipline (explicit)
- Always evaluate whether meaningful technical learnings occurred.
- When relevant, use the `learnings` skill to append entries in `docs/learnings/*`.

## 3) Commit cadence discipline (review-gated)
- Complete implementation, validation, and durable learning capture before the Fire review gate.
- Stage the complete candidate and do not commit until the review gate passes.

## 4) Final quality gate before push/finish (must pass)

- Run build/typecheck when configured.
